    Part 2: Simulation via numerical integration

    Parameter Tuning

    Effects of changing the spring constant

    • With a very low \(k_s\) (e.g., 50 \(N/m\)), the springs are weak and offer little resistance, causing the cloth to stretch significantly like a loose fabric as it drops; in its final resting state, it forms a very deep sagging curve between the two pinned points, and the overall mesh appears noticeably distorted downwards.
    • Using the default \(k_s\) (5000 \(N/m\)) provides a balanced simulation where the cloth falls and settles with a natural-looking curve, resembling typical fabrics like cotton.
    • Conversely, when the spring constant is set to a very high value (e.g., 50000 \(N/m\)), the springs become stiff. During the drop, the cloth holds its shape rigidly and exhibits fast micro-vibrations, and at rest, it hardly stretches at all, with the top edge remaining much straighter.

    Effects of changing the density

    • With a very low density (e.g., \(1 g/cm^2\)), the gravitational force is weak relative to the spring stiffness, causing the cloth to barely sag; it holds its shape rigidly, and the top edge remains nearly straight.
    • Using the default density (\(15 g/cm^2\)) yields a balanced result with a natural, moderate sag typical of regular fabrics.
    • In contrast, when the density is set very high (e.g., \(100 g/cm^2\)), the cloth becomes extremely heavy, and the strong downward pull of gravity easily overcomes the spring resistance. This results in the cloth stretching significantly as it drops, and in its final resting state, it forms a deep U-shaped droop between the pinned points.

    Effects of changing the damping

    • With very low damping (e.g., \(0\%\)), the cloth retains almost all of its momentum; as it drops, it swings back and forth with chaotic ripples.
    • Under the default damping (\(0.2\%\)), the cloth loses energy at a realistic rate, exhibiting a natural slight swing before smoothly coming to a steady rest.
    • In contrast, with a very high damping value (e.g., \(1.0\%\)), the cloth falls incredibly slowly and settles directly into its final shape without any swinging.
